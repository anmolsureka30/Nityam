# Rolling Motion Velocity of Topmost Point
`rolling_motion_velocity_of_topmost_point`


## Taught in shruti:cecb347d78_77f6cb3e @9:15
A kinematic principle stating that for a body rolling without slipping at a center-of-mass velocity v, the topmost point of the body moves with a linear speed of 2v relative to the ground. This concept is used to find the initial velocity of a detached particle from a rolling wheel in moving frame projectile problems.
