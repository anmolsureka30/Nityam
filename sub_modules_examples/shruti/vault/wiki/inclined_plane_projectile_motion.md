# Inclined Plane Projectile Motion
`inclined_plane_projectile_motion`


## Taught in shruti:a070d0331a_77f6cb3e @20:11
A technique for analyzing projectile motion on a sloped surface by setting the coordinate axes along the incline (x) and perpendicular to the incline (y), which decomposes the gravitational acceleration into a_x = g*sin(theta) and a_y = -g*cos(theta). In the lecture's example, a ball is projected normally (perpendicularly) from a 37-degree incline at 20 m/s, meaning u_x = 0 and u_y = 20 m/s. The time of flight is calculated as T = 2*u_y / a_y = 2*(20)/8 = 5 seconds, which is then used to calculate the range along the incline using R = 1/2 * a_x * T^2 = 1/2 * 6 * 25 = 75 meters.

- [text] Q. For a proj. eq. of traj. is
- [equation] y = x - x^2
- [text] Find max height & range of proj.
- [equation] H = \frac{u^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g} = \frac{10 \times \frac{1}{2}}{2 \times 10} = \frac{1}{4} = 0.25 \text{ m}
- [equation] R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g} = \frac{10}{10} = 1 \text{ m}
