# Generic Two-Dimensional Motion Analysis
`generic_two-dimensional_motion_analysis`


## Taught in shruti:a070d0331a_77f6cb3e @3:22
A method where two-dimensional motion is analyzed by splitting it into independent horizontal (x) and vertical (y) one-dimensional component equations linked by a common time variable t. In the lecture, this is demonstrated using a car with position vector r(t) = bt i_cap + ct^2 j_cap, from which the x and y coordinates are separated as x = bt and y = ct^2. By eliminating t (t = x/b), the parabolic equation of trajectory is derived as y = c*(x/b)^2, and the velocity and acceleration vectors are differentiated to find the angle between them using dot products or trigonometry.
