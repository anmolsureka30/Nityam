# Moving Frame Projectile Motion
`moving_frame_projectile_motion`


## Taught in shruti:a070d0331a_77f6cb3e @13:27
An analysis of a projectile launched from a moving frame, where the frame's velocity modifies the projectile's initial velocity vector in the ground frame. This is illustrated by a dust particle detaching from the top of a rolling rear wheel of radius a on a carriage moving forward at velocity v. Since the topmost point of a rolling wheel moves at 2v relative to the ground, the particle's launch velocity relative to the carriage is 2v - v = v. Using the relative frame, the particle travels a horizontal range R = sqrt(c^2 + (a-b)^2) and falls a vertical height h = 2(a-b) to reach the top of the front wheel of radius b separated by axle distance c.
