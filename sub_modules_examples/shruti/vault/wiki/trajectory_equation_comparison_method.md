# Trajectory Equation Comparison Method
`trajectory_equation_comparison_method`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @16:09
A method to extract physical parameters of a projectile, such as its launch angle θ and initial velocity u, by comparing a given trajectory equation to the standard form y = x tanθ - gx^2 / (2u^2 cos^2θ). For instance, comparing y = x - x^2 yields tanθ = 1 (giving θ = 45°) and g / (2u^2 cos^2θ) = 1 (giving u = √10 m/s), which are then used to compute the maximum height H = 0.25 m and range R = 1 m.
