# Rolling Body Topmost Point Velocity Rule
`rolling_body_topmost_point_velocity_rule`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @6:44
A kinematic principle stating that the topmost point of a pure rolling body moves with a horizontal velocity that is twice the velocity of its center of mass (2v). In the lecture, this rule is utilized to determine the initial projectile velocity of a dust particle detaching from the top of a tractor's rear wheel, showing that it detaches with a forward velocity of 2v relative to the ground.

- [text] Sol. Equation of traj.

- [equation] y = x - x^2
- [equation] \theta = 45^\circ
- [text] Q. For a proj. eq^n of traj
- [equation] y = x - x^2
- [text] Find max height & range of proj.
- [equation] \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g} = \frac{10 \times 1/2}{2 \times 10} = 1/4 = 0.25\text{ m} = H
- [equation] \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g} = \frac{10}{10} = 1\text{ m} = R
