# Vector Resolution
`vector_resolution`


## Taught in shruti:d_jnekwca6i_4c5411d0 @0:00
The process of breaking a single vector, such as the initial launch velocity of a projectile, into perpendicular components along different axes. For a projectile kicked with speed 'u' at an angle 'theta' from the ground, the velocity is split into a horizontal component of 'u cosine theta' and a vertical component of 'u sine theta', allowing each direction of motion to be analyzed independently.
