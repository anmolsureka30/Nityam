# Staircase Projectile Analysis
`staircase_projectile_analysis`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @3:01
To analyze when a horizontally projected projectile with speed u will collide with the edge of a step on a staircase of step width b and step height h, the coordinates of the nth step are represented as (nb, -nh). Substituting these coordinates directly into the horizontally projected trajectory equation y = -gx² / 2u² yields the equation -nh = -g(nb)² / 2u². This simplifies to find the exact step number n of the collision as n = 2hu² / gb².
