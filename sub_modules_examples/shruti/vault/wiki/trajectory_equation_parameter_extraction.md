# Trajectory Equation Parameter Extraction
`trajectory_equation_parameter_extraction`


## Taught in shruti:cecb347d78_77f6cb3e @19:40
A technique where the initial speed u and projection angle theta are determined by comparing a given trajectory equation y = Ax - Bx^2 to the standard projectile trajectory equation y = x * tan(theta) - gx^2 / (2u^2 * cos^2(theta)). Equating the coefficients yields tan(theta) = A and g / (2u^2 * cos^2(theta)) = B, which can be solved to find all other motion parameters such as maximum height and range.
