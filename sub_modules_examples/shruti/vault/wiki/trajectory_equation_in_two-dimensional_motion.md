# Trajectory Equation in Two-Dimensional Motion
`trajectory_equation_in_two-dimensional_motion`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @3:01
In generic two-dimensional motion, the trajectory equation relates the vertical coordinate y to the horizontal coordinate x by eliminating the common variable of time t. For a car with position coordinates x = bt and y = ct², substituting t = x/b yields the parabolic path y = c(x/b)². In projectile motion, comparing a given trajectory equation like y = x - x² with the standard equation y = x tan θ - gx² / (2u² cos² θ) allows the direct calculation of parameters like the launch angle (θ = 45°) and initial velocity (u = √10 m/s).
