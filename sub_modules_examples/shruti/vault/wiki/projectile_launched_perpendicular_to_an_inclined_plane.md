# Projectile Launched Perpendicular to an Inclined Plane
`projectile_launched_perpendicular_to_an_inclined_plane`


## Taught in shruti:cecb347d78_77f6cb3e @13:57
A specific case of inclined plane projectile motion where a projectile is launched perpendicular to an incline of angle theta. The acceleration components are resolved as ax = g * sin(theta) (parallel) and ay = g * cos(theta) (perpendicular), resulting in a time of flight of T = 2uy / ay and an incline range of R = 1/2 * ax * T^2, since the initial velocity component parallel to the incline is zero.
