# Perpendicular Condition for Projectile Velocity Vectors
`perpendicular_condition_for_projectile_velocity_vectors`


## Taught in shruti:cecb347d78_77f6cb3e @15:56
A method to find the time at which a projectile's instantaneous velocity vector becomes perpendicular to its launch velocity vector by setting their dot product to zero. Resolving the initial and instantaneous velocity vectors into components as u = ux i_cap + uy j_cap and v(t) = ux i_cap + (uy - gt) j_cap allows solving directly for the time t when the dot product is zero.
