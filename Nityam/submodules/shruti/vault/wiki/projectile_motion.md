## Taught in shruti:d_jnekwca6i_4c5411d0 @0:00
The motion of an object projected into the air that is subject only to the acceleration of gravity. This motion is analyzed by splitting it into two independent components: a horizontal component that maintains a constant speed, and a vertical component that undergoes constant downward acceleration under gravity, moving upward, momentarily stopping, and then coming back down.
