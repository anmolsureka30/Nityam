## Taught in shruti:d_jnekwca6i_4c5411d0 @2:00
The total duration that a projectile remains in the air from the moment of launch until it returns to the ground. It is derived using vertical kinematic equations, setting the final vertical velocity at the highest point to zero to find the ascent time, and then doubling it to obtain the full equation, 'T = 2 * u * sine(theta) / g'.
