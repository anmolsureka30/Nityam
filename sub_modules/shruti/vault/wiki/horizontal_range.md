# Horizontal Range
`horizontal_range`


## Taught in shruti:d_jnekwca6i_4c5411d0 @3:40
The total horizontal distance traveled by a projectile from its launch point until it returns to its initial horizontal plane. Because there is no horizontal acceleration under gravity, this distance is calculated by multiplying the constant horizontal speed component, 'u cosine theta', by the total time of flight, which simplifies to 'u squared times sine of 2 theta divided by g'.

- [equation] R = u \cos \theta \times \frac{2 u \sin \theta}{g}
- [equation] R = \frac{u^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
