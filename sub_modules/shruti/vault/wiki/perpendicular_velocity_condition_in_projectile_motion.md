# Perpendicular Velocity Condition in Projectile Motion
`perpendicular_velocity_condition_in_projectile_motion`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @8:45
An analytical approach to determine the time t at which a projectile's velocity vector v(t) becomes perpendicular to its initial velocity vector u. By setting their dot product to zero, u · v(t) = 0, where u = u_x i + u_y j and v(t) = u_x i + (u_y - gt) j, one can solve directly for the time. In the example, a projectile thrown with u = 55√3 i + 55 j m/s is shown to have its velocity vector perpendicular to u at t = 22 s.

**Common mistake (in the teacher's own words):**
> "So shall we take 2v? No, we shall take 2v - v because कैरिज भी v से चल रही है तो कैरिज के रिलेटिव ये v से ही चलेगा।"

When a projectile is launched from a moving frame, its velocity relative to that frame must be used to calculate its relative range, rather than its absolute ground velocity. **Correct understanding:** The velocity of the detached particle relative to the carriage (which travels at velocity v) is 2v - v = v, which is the correct velocity to use for calculating the relative range.
