# Impact Angle Condition in 2D Motion
`impact_angle_condition_in_2d_motion`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @20:11
The angle at which a projectile impacts a boundary is determined by the ratio of its vertical and horizontal velocity components, expressed as tan θ = vy / vx. If a projectile launched from a tower hits the ground at a 45° angle after 5 seconds, the relation tan 45° = 1 dictates that the final horizontal velocity vx and vertical velocity vy must be equal in magnitude. Equating vx = u cos 30° and vy = -(u sin 30° - gt) permits the calculation of the initial velocity u = 100 / (√3 + 1) m/s, which can be substituted into the vertical displacement equation to solve for the tower's height h.
