# Perpendicularity Condition for Velocity Vectors
`perpendicularity_condition_for_velocity_vectors`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @14:08
If two velocity vectors are mutually perpendicular, their dot product must be equal to zero. This principle is utilized to find the time t when a projectile's velocity vector v(t) becomes perpendicular to its initial launch velocity vector u. For a ball thrown at 110 m/s at 60° to the vertical, setting the dot product of u = 55√3 î + 55 ĵ and v(t) = 55√3 î + (55 - 10t) ĵ to zero results in a linear equation that yields the exact time of perpendicularity at t = 22 seconds.
