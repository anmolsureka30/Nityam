# Topmost Point Velocity of a Rolling Body
`topmost_point_velocity_of_a_rolling_body`


## Taught in shruti:52f8e6f7ad_77f6cb3e @9:25
For any rolling body moving with a center-of-mass velocity v, the linear velocity of its topmost point is twice that of its center, or 2v. In the case of a dust particle detaching from the top of a carriage's rear wheel of radius a moving forward at speed v, the particle initiates projectile motion with a velocity of 2v. This results in a relative speed of v with respect to the carriage, allowing the particle to cover a relative horizontal range of √(c² + (a - b)²) while falling a relative height of 2(a - b) to land on the front wheel of radius b.
